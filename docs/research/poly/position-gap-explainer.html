position_gap — the 60-second version

North star, one knob, what's deliberately missing.

North star

Hold a miniature of target's BOOK. As they grow / shrink / rotate, our positions track theirs in proportion — scaled to our budget.

TL;DR — 2026-05-18 redesign

  • target_scaleOpaque 0.0005-style fraction dropped. PR #102 shipped the wrong abstraction; pre-prod policy lets us purge cleanly.
  • mirror_capital_alloc_usdcOne knob, in dollars: "I'm betting $C of my book tracking this target's whole book."
  • no per-trade capmirror_max_usdc_per_trade is an anti-pattern under book matching — it throttles tracking. position_gap ignores it; poly_wallet_grants is the wire-level safety net.

The math

Every target fill is a re-evaluation trigger, not a sizing input. Fill arrives → re-derive scale → re-derive gap → close it.

scale          = capital_alloc_usdc / Σ target_total_open_book_cost_usdc
desired_shares = target_shares × scale            (per token target holds)
gap_shares     = desired_shares − our_shares
intent_usdc    = gap_shares × fill.price
                 ↓ clamp to market floor only
PLACE or SKIP

Layering falls out for free (gap shrinks as we fill). Minority-side fills collapse below market min and skip — the Sinner/Ruud inverted-weighting bug is structurally impossible.

🔴 SKIP below_market_min

Implicit conviction threshold derived for free: target_position_threshold = market_min × target_book / alloc = $1 × $50k / $50 = $1,000. Target positions below $1k get skipped automatically, no explicit filter needed. Threshold floats as target's book grows / alloc changes.

The invariant: our desired position, valued at target's per-share cost, stays at a fixed fraction (alloc / target_book) of target's. As target concentrates → we concentrate. As they diversify → we diversify. As they halve → we halve.

What's deliberately NOT a knob

  • No min_target_position_usdc floor. Polymarket's per-market min_usdc_notional + the derived threshold formula above already handle this. Adding a hand-set $-floor would duplicate one of those two mechanisms.
  • No mirror_max_usdc_per_trade for position_gap. The whole point of book-matching is to track target proportionally. A per-trade cap would throttle that tracking. Wire-level safety is the grant's job, not the policy's. (Column stays on the row for legacy policies that still use it; position_gap just doesn't read it.)
  • No percentile filter. target_percentile_scaled's pXX is a legacy conviction model ("small for THEM = noise"); position_gap's conviction model is mechanical ("too small for US to economically mirror"). The derived threshold IS the filter. Keep both policies side-by-side until A/B tells us which model wins.

Cross-target portfolio cap = on-chain poly_wallet_grants.total_at_risk_usdc trips. Cross-target velocity = poly_wallet_grants.daily_cap_usdc. Per-fill blast radius: lower bound = market's structural floor (min_usdc_notional); upper bound = none at the policy layer. Planner passes +Infinity as the ceiling to applyMarketFloors so per-fill intent can exceed alloc when target averages up past their cost-basis VWAP. The grant is the only cross-fill stop. The policy stays clean; safety lives in the grant.

The tradeoff (read this before you ship)

Dollar-anchored proportional sizing has a real property to understand:

🟢 risk discipline🟡 anti-conviction in trending winners
As target's VWAP rises, our share count shrinks. We hold less per share than target as they average up — we can't bag-hold worse than target's own cost curve. When target adds conviction at higher prices in a trending move (their thesis still holds), we mirror with progressively fewer shares. We trail trending winners.

Whether this net-wins depends on whether target's late-stage conviction adds are informed (we should over-weight, this policy under-weights) or revert (we should hold dollar value steady, this policy does). v1 picks the latter. Revisit if A/B shows we trail trending winners more than we save on falling knives.

What still bites (flag, do not ship blind)

  • Σ requires target's WHOLE book, not just the current condition. v0 needs a cached per-target full-positions fetch (reuse listAllUserPositions, TTL ~30s). New dep, but cheap.
  • Σ = 0 needs an explicit guard. Target closes everything between snapshot and fill, or live read fails — planner skips target_position_below_threshold, never divides by zero.
  • VWAP gate is per-fill, not per-order. vwap_floor_breach compares fill.price to target's residual cost-basis per fill. Phase 4's GapExecutor promotes this to limit_price = min(fill.price, target_vwap + ε).
  • Static $ alloc, not %. Alloc doesn't auto-grow / shrink with our wallet equity. Tenant rebalances manually. v1 (filed as follow-up) switches to mirror_capital_alloc_pct of wallet equity — fractional-Kelly framing, auto-throttling. Requires a wallet-equity read path; deferred.
  • Account balance < alloc. Planner stays naive (doesn't read balance). Executor wire fails INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE; placements pause until cash frees up via redeem/SELL/deposit. Clean separation.
  • Multi-target same-market collision. Two targets long different sides of the same binary — each computes its scale independently against the same our_shares. Evaluation order determines allocation. Phase 3+ cross-target reconciler.
