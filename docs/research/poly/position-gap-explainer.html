position_gap — the 60-second version

What the policy does, the math, what we're testing, where to look for more.

What it does

The legacy policy sizes each target fill in isolation, which inverts the mirror when target's position is lopsided. position_gap instead sizes from the gap between target's current position and ours. Every fill is a re-evaluation trigger, not a sizing input.

desired_shares = target_shares × target_scale
gap_shares     = desired_shares − our_shares
intent_usdc    = gap_shares × fill.price
                 ↓ clamp to market floors & per-leg cap
PLACE or SKIP

Layering falls out for free (gap shrinks as we fill). Minority-side fills collapse below market min and skip — the Sinner/Ruud inverted-weighting bug is structurally impossible under this policy.

The one real algorithmic gap to flag

VWAP is not first-class on either side today. The vwap_floor_breach gate compares fill.price against target's residual cost-basis (derived per-fill from a Polymarket Data-API snapshot). Our own VWAP is tracked in the mirror cache but unused under position_gap. Phase 4's GapExecutor promotes both to first-class fields backed by chain truth and shifts the constraint onto the order's limit_price instead of skipping per-fill.

Until Phase 4: a half-day patch can backport that mechanism to position_gap alone — cap intent.limit_price = min(fill.price, target_vwap + ε), drop the per-fill skip. Decide after the 24h A/B data lands.
