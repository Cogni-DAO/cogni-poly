🔎 Finding detail

What: Over 4 days swisstony cleared +$136,633 (+1.73%) on $7.88M of buys across 3,255 markets (2,507 wins / 2,252 losses on 2,783 resolved). We tested 10 paper algorithms against him; 0 of 10 made money. The closest paper variant — SWISSTONY_BUDGET_MIRROR (position_gap @ $500k, sized to swisstony's actual book) — managed −1.22%; the worst (auto p75/$5 duplicates on candidate-a) hit −3.21%. The result is uniform across every sizing policy and cap_alloc from $5 to $500k.

Why: Two compounding causes. (1) The planner is fill-shaped — nodes/poly/app/src/features/copy-trade/plan-mirror.ts:323 — planMirrorFromFill reacts to each of swisstony's fills as a fresh decision, so we enter at post-spike prices (D2 fill-chase per work/charters/POLY_COPY_DELTA.md:34). (2) The paper sidecar prices fills against a simulated orderbook — nodes/poly/sidecars/paper-trader/server.py:490 — engine.place_limit_order — that doesn't reproduce real CLOB queue dynamics. We CAN'T quantify (2) in isolation right now: Q1 = ⚪ NOT TESTABLE because prod LIVE has 0 decisions in window (copy-trade disabled on derek's prod wallet). Until prod resumes AND a policy-matched fidelity twin exists, the paper-vs-live adapter question is open.

Next fix: Two parallel moves. (a) Ship position-mirror Phase 3 per docs/spec/poly-copy-trade-position-mirror.md — dissolves D2/D3/D4 jointly by making the planner position-shaped instead of fill-shaped; this is the highest-leverage architectural fix and doesn't require Q1 to be answerable first.
