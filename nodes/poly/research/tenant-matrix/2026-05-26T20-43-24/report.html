Why this isn't a sample-size or policy-tuning problem. The matrix spans three sizing policies (auto/$15 legacy, position_gap @ $1k / $50k / $500k allocations) and two envs. Every variant is negative. Scaling cap_alloc from $1k → $500k doesn't flip the sign — it just scales the loss. swisstony's positive PnL is on the same on-chain fills (same markets, same outcomes). The disagreement is on PnL sign at the per-market level, which means the divergence is on entry price (VWAP), not on market selection.

D2 fill-chase is the structural cause, per work/charters/POLY_COPY_DELTA.md: "We react to target's latest fill, which clusters at price spikes. Target's true position has good VWAP because they built quietly; our reactive VWAP is bad by construction." The planner entry point is fill-shaped: nodes/poly/app/src/features/copy-trade/plan-mirror.ts:323 — planMirrorFromFill(input: PlanMirrorInput): MirrorPlan. Each fill is a fresh decision; there is no concept of "target's current position vs ours" that would let the mirror average down or skip already-bad-VWAP entries. The previously committed finding (a94309c65) framed the same data as "position_gap loses 2.5x auto's PnL-rate"; reframed against the target's own truth, the sharper claim is that no sizing policy on the current fill-shaped planner produces positive PnL against a clearly-profitable target.
