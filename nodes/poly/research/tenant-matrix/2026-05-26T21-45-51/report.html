🔎 Finding detail

What: Over the last 4 days swisstony cleared +$133,259 (+1.69%) on $7.86M of buys across 3,218 markets. We tested 10 paper algorithms against him; every single one lost money, between −0.7% and −3.2%. The best paper variant (position_gap @ $500k, sized to swisstony's actual book) only managed −1.22%. This is not a tuning problem — adding three more DB-only variants (376c594c, fb8f65d5, plus three duplicate p75/$5 rows) since the prior run confirmed the sign flip is uniform across auto and position_gap, $5 cap to $500k cap.

Why: Two compounding causes. (1) The planner is fill-shaped — nodes/poly/app/src/features/copy-trade/plan-mirror.ts:323 — planMirrorFromFill reacts to each of swisstony's fills as a fresh decision, so we enter at post-spike prices (D2 fill-chase per work/charters/POLY_COPY_DELTA.md:34). (2) The paper sidecar prices fills against a simulated orderbook — nodes/poly/sidecars/paper-trader/server.py:490 — engine.place_limit_order — that doesn't reproduce real Polymarket CLOB queue dynamics, so paper PnL is biased low even when the policy is reasonable. The Q1 twin (legacy auto p80/$15) is intent-capped — its size gap dominates its distance number — but the size-matched position_gap @ $500k variant removes that excuse and still loses money. So the residual signal is architectural, not undersizing.

Next fix: Two parallel moves. (a) Ship position-mirror Phase 3 per docs/spec/poly-copy-trade-position-mirror.md — dissolves D2/D3/D4 jointly by making the planner position-shaped rather than fill-shaped. (b) Provision a true fidelity trust twin: POLY_PROD_TENANT_TRUST_TWIN configured with the identical sizing policy + cap_alloc as prod LIVE so future Q1 measures paper-vs-live adapter fidelity in isolation (instead of mixing it with policy-undersizing as today). Until both ship, treat any paper-side ranking as advisory — no live-money promotion. Tuning mirror_capital_alloc_usdc further on the current planner is wasted motion.
