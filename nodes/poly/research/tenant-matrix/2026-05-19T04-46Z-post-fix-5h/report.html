What is NOT the cause: not a paper-sidecar fill bug. twin_only_fills=0 and live_only_fills=0 — the sidecar sees every fill the CLOB sees. bug.5060's fill-capture direction is fixed. placement_failed=0 across all 6 tenants.

Actual cause: per-tenant position state. Planner gates followup_position_too_small, position_cap_reached, already_resting, and below_market_min all read the calling tenant's positions (plan-mirror.ts:218-247). LIVE's wallet carries months of derek-trading history; paper TRUST_TWIN's positions only reflect what it has placed since registration. Identical fills + different state ⇒ different decisions. 43 of the 46-placement gap = twin placed (layer_scale_in) where live skipped (followup_position_too_small or below_market_min).

The "trust twin" earned its keep: the instrument's job is to falsify the assumption that paper tracks live. It did exactly that. Don't rename it — heed it.
